01How to enter

  1. Email siddharthanhiremath@gmail.com for an account.
  2. Log in on the race site and click My script. You get a working starter bot you can deploy straight away.
  3. Hit Deploy (or use Upload .py). Your 3 cars leave the pits and start racing.
  4. Improve it and redeploy as often as you like. Each deploy hot-swaps the code on all 3 cars mid-lap. Laps, positions and tyres carry over.

02What your bot does

Your file defines one function. It gets everything the car can see and returns what the car should do:

def drive(state):
    return {
        "throttle": 1.0,   # -1..1
        "brake":    0.0,   # 0..1
        "steer":    0.0,   # -1..1  (+ = left)
        "drs":      0,     # 0..100: faster on straights, less grip in corners
        "pit":      False, # True = pit next time past the entry
        "tyre":     "medium",  # soft | medium | hard, fitted at your next stop
    }

state includes your car (speed, position, tyre wear, damage), every other car, a 36-ray lidar scan, the next 400 m of track, the full map and the physics constants. The full list is at the top of the starter script.

  • state["me"]["car_number"] is 1, 2 or 3, so one script can give each car a different job.
  • Cars marked teammate in state["cars"] are yours.
  • print() output shows up in your car's log. Only you can see your logs and your code.
  • Python standard library and numpy are available. No network, no file access.

03Scoring

Every car scores points equal to how many places it is from last. Last place is worth 1 point, second-to-last 2, and so on. Your team score is the sum of your 3 cars.

Standings are live on the main page. The race has no scheduled end. Whatever the standings say when a race is called is the result.

04Race starts and qualifying

  • When a new race is started, there's a countdown and every car leaves its pit box at the same moment.
  • Some races open with qualifying: each team gets one out-lap and one flying lap on an empty track. That one time sets the grid for all 3 of your cars.
  • If you join while a race is running, your cars just head out from the pits and start racing.

05The physics, in one minute

  • Grip is limited. Ask for more than the tyres have and you slide, wear the tyres fast and maybe spin.
  • Braking and turning share grip. Braking hard mid-corner means less steering.
  • Downforce grows with speed, so fast corners can be taken faster than you'd expect.
  • Grass has half grip. Kerbs are slightly slippery. Walls hurt.
  • Tyres: softs grip most and wear fastest, hards last longest. Every compound falls off a cliff around 75% wear, so you have to pit.
  • Collisions are real. Car-to-car and wall hits push you around and cause damage, which slows you until you pit.
  • Pit stops are automatic once you request one: the crew drives you in, changes tyres, repairs damage and sends you back out.
  • A car that's stuck for 10 seconds gets towed back to its box.

06The rules

  1. One account per person. Don't share your login.
  2. Your car is driven by your script. Write it yourself or with any tools you like, AI included. Just no manual driving.
  3. Race the cars, not the server. Don't try to break out of the sandbox, crash or slow the server, or read other players' code or data. Doing so gets your account removed.
  4. Contact is racing. The physics decides what happens in a collision. Hard racing is fine. If something looks broken, tell me rather than exploiting it.
  5. Bugs get fixed. If you find a physics or server bug, report it. Fixes may change the sim mid-race; it's the same for everyone.
  6. Keep it under 200 KB. That's the max script size.
  7. The organizer's call is final on disputes, restarts and when a race ends.

07Tips to get started

  • Deploy the starter bot first. It works, and you'll learn a lot just watching it.
  • Click your car on the main page to see its telemetry, onboard camera and lidar.
  • Going fast alone is half the problem. Getting past other cars without crashing is the other half.
  • Plan your pit stops. Running past the tyre cliff costs far more than a stop.
